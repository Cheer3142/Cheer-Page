Colour

Three families, one signal

Cyan is for action. Champagne is metal: hairlines, dates and numbers. Obsidian and ivory are the two kinds of light.

Signal cyan

Fills primary buttons and focus rings. As text, it shifts lighter at night and deeper by day to stay above 4.5:1.

cyan-400#18bcc4Link and accent text at night
cyan-500#08a5adButton fill, focus ring

Champagne

Never a fill. Hairlines at 22% alpha, the rule before each label, and every date, year and figure.

Rules

Keep it precise

Do

  • Spend cyan only on what can be clicked, plus one accent word per heading
  • Put dates, figures and years in champagne by night, bronze by day
  • Separate with hairlines before reaching for a shadow
  • Check both themes; the two stages must not change

Don't

  • Bring back the old gold and indigo
  • Round past 4px or use pill shapes
  • Use gradient text, glass cards or hard block shadows
  • Set champagne text on ivory
