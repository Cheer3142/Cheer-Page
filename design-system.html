Keep it precise

Do

  • Spend cyan on actions, live events and one headline letter
  • Alternate charcoal and fog, joined by the notch
  • Set Latin statements at weight 400 with negative tracking
  • Remap tokens locally with .dark and .card-dark

Don't

  • Bring back champagne, ivory, gold or a day/night toggle
  • Put a label above a heading
  • Shadow text cards or round past 14px
  • Use gradient text or glass cards
